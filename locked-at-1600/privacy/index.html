Locked at 1600: privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Locked at 1600 ("the game") is a single-player puzzle game for Android, made by Velfield. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or device information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone only: which locks you have opened, which clues are in your journal and your settings, such as day or night. This data never leaves the device. To delete it, choose "New night" on the title screen or uninstall the game.

Permissions

The Android package lists the standard network permission that the Unity engine includes by default. The game does not use it: it makes no network connections. It may also use the phone's vibration motor for a short pulse when a lock opens.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example because a feature that needs data is added, the new version will be published at this address with a new date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to locked1600@velfield.com.
