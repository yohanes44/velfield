Who we are

This website, velfield.com, is run by Velfield LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company and software studio. You can reach us at hello@velfield.com.

What this website collects

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising. We don't collect or store personal information through it.

Services this website uses

Like any website, loading these pages means your browser connects to the services that deliver them. The site's fonts and images are served from velfield.com itself. Each service receives your IP address and basic browser information as part of the request:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep server logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare provides the domain's DNS, which turns velfield.com into the site's address.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to work with you, and we don't sell or share it. Our email is hosted by Zoho Mail.

Our apps

This policy covers this website only. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, for example CrimeGhost's privacy policy and Locked at 1600's privacy policy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above.
