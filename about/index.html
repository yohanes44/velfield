About Us

One studio for the whole product.

Velfield is an independent software studio, founded in 2026. We build our own apps and games, and we build for clients.

Why "Velfield"

The name comes from physics. A velocity field describes how every part of a system moves, together.

That is how we think about software: the app, the website, the back-end and the integrations are one system, designed to move as one. So we take a product from the first idea to the live release, and keep it running after.

What we do

Velfield LLC develops and publishes software: mobile apps, websites and web applications, games, and back-end systems and integrations, for its own products and for clients.
