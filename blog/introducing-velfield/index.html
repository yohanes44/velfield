Introducing Velfield

Who we are, what we build, and why the studio is named after a velocity field.

Velfield is an independent software studio. We build mobile apps, websites and web applications, games, and the back-end systems and integrations that connect them, for our own products and for clients.

What we have built so far

InkConsent keeps consent records for tattoo studios. Clients sign on the studio's phone, on their own phone or on paper, and every consent is filed as a time-stamped PDF. It is live on Google Play.

CrimeGhost is a detective mystery for your phone: you unlock a missing person's phone or laptop, search their apps and messages, and work out what happened. It is coming to Google Play.

A third game, Locked at 1600, is in the works.

Why the name

A velocity field, in physics, describes how every part of a system moves together. We think about software the same way: the app, the site, the server and the integrations are one system, and we design them to move as one.

Working with us

If you have something you want built, write to hello@velfield.com with a few lines about the idea, who it is for and when you need it. We reply to every message.
